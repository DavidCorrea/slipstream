          Natural voices for the broadcast?

          The commentator, your driver and your engineer can speak in voices that sound like people. They're generated by a small AI model on your graphics card, in your browser: nothing is sent anywhere.

          A one-time download of about 325 MB; your browser keeps it for next time. You can change your mind in Settings.
